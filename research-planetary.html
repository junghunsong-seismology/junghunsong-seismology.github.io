Research 01

Planetary
seismology

I use seismic observations from rocky planetary bodies together with forward modelling and inverse methods to investigate seismic sources, crustal structure, and ongoing tectonic activity.

Current themes

Seismicity and structure beyond Earth

My current planetary work focuses on Mars and the Moon. A central goal is to extract as much physical information as possible from sparse seismic observations: source size and mechanism, propagation effects, crustal and mantle structure, and the distinction between tectonic and impact-generated signals.

Marsquake sources

Magnitude scaling, source characterisation, attenuation and structural effects, and comparison with impact-generated signals.

Valles Marineris tectonics

Waveform-based constraints on active crustal deformation and fault reactivation on Mars.

Moonquake location

Single-station approaches for constraining lunar event location and source geometry from sparse observations.

Planetary crustal structure

Receiver functions, surface waves, transdimensional inversion, and forward modelling for Mars interior structure.
