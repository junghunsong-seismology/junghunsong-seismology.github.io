Research 02

Earthquake
source physics

I investigate how seismic waveforms encode source mechanism, moment, depth, rupture properties, and the interaction between source processes and three-dimensional structure.

Approach

Source information from complete waveforms

My source studies combine moment-tensor analysis, forward wavefield simulations, waveform fitting, and source-scaling relationships. I am particularly interested in situations where structural complexity, sparse station coverage, or non-standard sources make conventional earthquake analysis difficult.

Moment tensors

Mechanism, depth, and uncertainty from waveform-based inversion using realistic velocity structure.

Magnitude scaling

Calibration of seismic source size while accounting for propagation, attenuation, and structural response.

3-D waveform simulation

Testing how heterogeneous Earth structure modifies observed waveforms and inferred source parameters.

Planetary sources

Extending source-physics methods to marsquakes, impacts, and tectonic activity on Mars.

Conceptual workflow: waveform observations are compared with numerical simulations to constrain source parameters and their uncertainties.
