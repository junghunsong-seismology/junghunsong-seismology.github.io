Research 03

Seismic imaging
& wavefields

I use seismic tomography, numerical wave propagation, finite-frequency sensitivity, and inverse methods to connect observed waveforms with structure from the crust to the core–mantle boundary.

Research scope

Imaging structure across scales

My imaging work spans lithospheric structure beneath intraplate volcanic systems, regional crust and upper mantle structure, mantle-transition-zone dynamics, and deep-mantle heterogeneity. Numerical wavefield simulations are used both as an imaging tool and as a way to understand how structural complexity maps into seismic observations.

Lithosphere & volcanism

Tomographic constraints on intraplate volcanic systems, lithosphere–asthenosphere interaction, and melt focusing.

Full wavefields

Three-dimensional spectral-element simulations for realistic propagation through heterogeneous structure.

Finite-frequency sensitivity

Wavefield kernels and waveform sensitivity to structures that cannot be treated as infinitesimal rays.

Deep mantle

Seismic constraints on mantle-transition-zone structure and lowermost-mantle heterogeneity near the CMB.
